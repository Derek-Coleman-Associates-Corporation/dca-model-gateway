# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **support@dcassociatesgroup.com** with `SECURITY` at the start of the subject line. Please include what you found, how
to reproduce it, and how we can reach you. Do not open a public issue for a vulnerability, and do not include anyone's
gateway key, Marketplace token or personal data.

We acknowledge every report and keep you informed until the issue is resolved.

## If your gateway key leaks

Choose **Regenerate gateway key** on the subscription's menu at <https://saas.dcassociatesgroup.com>. The old key stops
working immediately. To stop all calls to your Foundry at once, select **Disconnect** on the Connect your Foundry page. Removing
the DCA Model Gateway Connector's role on your resource blocks it in Azure as well, once Azure applies the change.
